Grinnell Glacier

Cliffs, waterfalls, wildlife country, and an ever-widening view over the Many Glacier valley before the final arrival at Upper Grinnell Lake.

Distance
About 10.6 miles
Style
Out & back

Trail totals vary slightly by recording and access. Allow roughly 23–24 miles for the full day.

Three days / two nights

A weekend with one wild center.

  1. 01
    Friday · August 6

    Arrive & exhale

    Fly Phoenix to Kalispell, collect the rental car, and make the scenic drive into Many Glacier. Check in, share an early dinner, lay out tomorrow's kit, and get some sleep.

  2. 02
    Saturday · August 7

    Chase the blue

    Start early for Cracker Lake. Return to Many Glacier for food, water, and a quick reset, then head back out for Grinnell Glacier. Finish the day with dinner and very tired legs.

  3. 03
    Sunday · August 8

    Slow morning, homeward

    Sleep in, enjoy breakfast and one last mountain view, then drive back to Kalispell for the return flight to Phoenix.
