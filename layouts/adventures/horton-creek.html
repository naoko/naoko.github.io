02 / 🌟 Recommended · 7-mile jog/hike

Horton Spring

This is what we’re walking for: little cascades threading between mossy rocks, clear pools and green woodland along the creek. Our recommended seven-mile jog/hike scenario includes about 1,300 feet of estimated elevation gain and 20 minutes total for water, a snack and photos before heading back for lunch.

Follow Horton Creek toward Horton Spring and return the same way. Travelness publishes 8.6 miles, but its plotted track continues beyond OpenStreetMap’s spring marker. Along that track, the closest point to the mapped spring is about 3.43 miles from the route start, roughly 155 feet from the marker. Doubling that segment gives 6.85 miles before the unverified spring approach and parking connection. The roughly 1,300-foot gain is a terrain-model estimate for the measured out-and-back near the spring. The final approach and parking connection are not fully captured. This is coordinate evidence, not a verified car-to-spring route. Park at Derrick Trailhead for this plan and allow about five minutes each way to Horton Creek Trailhead.

Understand the climb

Creek climb, then a steeper finish.

When would we get near the spring? The measured 3.43-mile outbound segment takes about 1 hr 09 min at 20 min/mile, or 1 hr 43 min at 30 min/mile. Add five minutes from parking and ten minutes of pauses: leaving the car at 8:20 puts you near the mapped spring around 9:44 am or 10:18 am, respectively. Reaching the water may take longer because the final approach has not been measured.

Start → spring vicinity

~1,280 ft

Net climb over ~3.43 miles

Roughly 5,460 to 6,740 feet. About 7% average uphill grade. Expect the outbound miles to take more effort than the downhill return; use a comfortable hiking pace on the climbs.

Beyond spring vicinity

~1,150 ft

More climb in ~0.88 mile

The full mapped route continues to roughly 7,890 feet. Its net rise is about 25% of horizontal distance over this segment. This continuation is much steeper; a 20-minute mile here may be unrealistic.

🌟 Recommended base · 7-mile jog/hike

One day, in order.

Leave the Phoenix area at 5:20 am, jog/hike at a moving average of 20 min/mile, and allow 20 minutes total for breaks. This seven-mile scenario has about 1,300 feet of estimated gain. Target an 11:00 return to the car, followed by lunch around noon. This schedule has no extra hiking buffer.

  • 5:20 am

    Leave Phoenix

    Budget 2 hr 30 min driving and a 15-minute charging allowance. Aim for the Payson stop around 7:10–7:25 am, then continue to Derrick parking. Coffee and a restroom visit can overlap charging if service is quick; extra time comes from the morning buffer. Starting the Model Y at 100% may let you skip that charging stop if navigation confirms enough reserve. Your actual starting point and traffic determine the drive.

  • ~7:10 am

    Charge & coffee in Payson

    Budget 15 minutes charging at the Payson Supercharger. The Starbucks by Bashas’ is about two minutes away on foot and lists a restroom. Coffee and bathroom time can overlap charging if the counter is open and service is quick; allow extra if needed. See the stop, walking map and hours ↓

  • 8:05 am

    Park at Derrick

    Take 15 minutes to change shoes, organize water and get ready. The connection to Horton Creek Trailhead is included in the seven-mile car-to-car planning budget.

  • 8:20 am

    Jog/hike to the spring

    Seven miles at 20 min/mile is 2 hr 20 moving. Add 20 minutes total for pauses, a snack and photos: 2 hr 40 elapsed. Hike the climbs and jog comfortable sections. This average and the seven-mile spring distance are planning assumptions, not promises.

  • 11:00 am

    Back at the car

    Target finish for the base plan. Change shoes and head toward Payson promptly. There is no spare hiking buffer before the lunch drive; a longer spring approach, slower pace or extra stop shifts lunch later. Turn back early if the remaining route will exceed the window.

  • 11:00–noon

    Drive to Payson

    Allow about 40 minutes driving to Duza’s Kitchen plus 20 minutes for parking, a possible wait and delays. Derrick Trailhead → Duza’s directions ↗. This is a planning allowance; check the actual route and traffic.

  • Noon

    Lunch at Duza’s Kitchen

    Block 60–75 minutes, through ordering, eating and paying. View Duza’s Kitchen ↗. Confirm current hours and kitchen cutoff; the provisional close is 2 pm. Macky’s Grill ↗ is the backup for a later lunch.

  • 1:00–1:15 pm

    Finish lunch & check charge

    If Tesla navigation calls for a top-up, budget 15 minutes charging plus up to ten minutes for a queue or detour. This is separate from lunch; restaurant charging is not confirmed. Without a stop, head home after lunch. With it, expect to leave Payson around 1:15–1:40. Allow longer if navigation predicts more charging.

  • 3:00–4:00 pm

    Home in Phoenix

    Allow roughly two hours driving from Payson to your Phoenix-area destination. A 1:00 departure puts you home around 3:00; a 2:00 departure around 4:00. These are provisional travel allowances, not live journey times.

Tesla plan · coffee & comfort stop

Plug in. Grab a coffee.

15 minutes charging. The itinerary assumes a Model Y starting at 100%, with a 15-minute top-up allowance in Payson. Actual need comes from Tesla navigation, not a fixed promise. Precondition using the charging destination and follow the car’s predicted duration.

Payson Supercharger. Google Maps lists it at 142 AZ-260, Payson Village, open 24 hours. Enter the Supercharger itself in navigation. Check current stall availability in the car; station power and live availability were not independently verified.

Coffee + restroom nearby. The Starbucks at the same 142 AZ-260 address is about a 2-minute walk / 456 feet from the chargers according to Google Maps, whose café listing includes an accessible restroom. Bashas’ official page lists the host store at this address open 6 am–9 pm every day.
