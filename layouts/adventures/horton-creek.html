02 / The spring goal

Horton Spring

The reward is seeing where the creek begins: cold water emerging from the mountainside, tumbling over rocks beneath the trees. It makes a lovely place to sit, eat a snack and listen to the water before heading back. The photograph shows one of two adjacent springs; flow and foliage will vary with the season.

Follow Horton Creek toward Horton Spring and return the same way. Travelness publishes 8.6 miles, but its plotted track continues beyond OpenStreetMap’s spring marker. Along that track, the closest point to the mapped spring is about 3.43 miles from the route start, roughly 155 feet from the marker. Doubling that segment gives 6.85 miles before the unverified spring approach and parking connection. This is coordinate evidence, not a verified car-to-spring route. Park at Derrick Trailhead for this plan and allow about five minutes each way to Horton Creek Trailhead.

Understand the climb

Creek climb, then a steeper finish.

When would we get near the spring? The measured 3.43-mile outbound segment takes about 1 hr 09 min at 20 min/mile, or 1 hr 43 min at 30 min/mile. Add five minutes from parking and 15 minutes of pauses: leaving the car at 7:10 puts you near the mapped spring around 8:39 am or 9:13 am, respectively. Reaching the water may take longer because the final approach has not been measured.

Start → spring vicinity

~1,280 ft

Net climb over ~3.43 miles

Roughly 5,460 to 6,740 feet. About 7% average uphill grade. Expect the outbound miles to take more effort than the downhill return; use a comfortable hiking pace on the climbs.

Beyond spring vicinity

~1,150 ft

More climb in ~0.88 mile

The full mapped route continues to roughly 7,890 feet. Its net rise is about 25% of horizontal distance over this segment. This continuation is much steeper; a 20-minute mile here may be unrealistic.

  1. 01
    About 4:00 am departure · full-route jog/hike

    Aim for 7:00 am parking

    Allow 2 hr 30 min driving plus a 30-minute charging allowance. Arrive at Derrick parking around 7:00, spend ten minutes getting ready, then leave on foot at 7:10. At an overall 20 min/mile, the 8.6-mile route plus 45 minutes of breaks and ten minutes of connection finishes around 10:57. The 9-mile buffer finishes around 11:05. This is an ambitious pace on the steep upper continuation.

  2. 02
    About 4:45 am departure · five-mile slow hike

    Aim for 7:45 am parking

    Use the same three-hour drive and charging allowance, then take 15 minutes to get ready. Walk from the car at 8:00. Five miles at 30 min/mile plus 30 minutes of breaks brings you back at 11:00. At 20 min/mile, the same outing finishes around 10:10, leaving room for a longer creek stop.

  3. 03
    7-mile spring scenario · provisional

    A middle option for the spring

    Assume seven miles total from Derrick parking and back, including the connection, plus 45 minutes for snacks, photos and spring time. At 20 min/mile, allow 3 hr 05 total: arrive at parking around 7:40, leave on foot at 7:50 and return about 10:55. At 30 min/mile, allow 4 hr 15: arrive around 6:30, leave on foot at 6:40 and return about 10:55. With the three-hour driving and charging allowance, that means Phoenix departures around 4:40 or 3:30 am, respectively.

    If you leave parking at 8:00 instead, seven miles plus the same breaks finishes at 11:05 am at jog/hike pace or 12:15 pm at slow-hike pace. Keeping the one-hour drive/arrival allowance puts lunch around 12:05 or 1:15. For noon lunch, turn around early if the route or pace exceeds the plan. Reaching Horton Spring within seven miles is not guaranteed.

  4. 04
    9:00 am parking · fallback

    Make it a shorter creek walk

    Allow ten minutes to get ready and leave the car at 9:10. Keep the turnaround to two miles at jog/hike pace, or about 1.3 miles at slow-hike pace, to return by 11:00 with 30 minutes of breaks. Completing 8.6 miles instead would finish around 12:57 at jog/hike pace or 2:23 at slow-hike pace, before the drive to lunch.

  5. 05
    Full route at slow-hike pace

    Earlier start or later lunch

    For the full 8.6-mile route at 30 min/mile, leave parking on foot by about 5:45 am to finish around 10:58. With ten minutes of setup and the three-hour drive/charge allowance, that means leaving Phoenix around 2:35 am. If that is too early, choose the five-mile plan or use Macky’s for a later lunch.

  6. 06
    11:00 am–noon · drive + arrival buffer

    Drive to lunch in Payson

    Leave Derrick Trailhead parking at 11:00. Budget about 40 minutes driving to Duza’s Kitchen, then 20 minutes for parking, a possible wait and delays. Aim to be seated by noon. This is a planning allowance; check the actual route and traffic before departure. Derrick Trailhead → Duza’s driving directions ↗

  7. 07
    Noon–1:00 / 1:15 pm · 60–75 minutes

    Lunch at Duza’s Kitchen

  8. 08

Keep this separate from the lunch block; restaurant charging has not been confirmed. Without charging, leave after lunch around 1:00–1:15. With it, allow departure closer to 1:30–2:00.

    Allow roughly two hours from Payson to your Phoenix-area destination. A 1:00 departure puts you home around 3:00; a 2:00 departure around 4:00. These are planning estimates, not checked live journey times. Adjust for your destination, traffic and any further stops.
