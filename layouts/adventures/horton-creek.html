Distance evidence and sources

USGS GNIS feature 30217 identifies the spring. The USFS trail record gives Horton Creek Trail #285 a GIS length of 3.425 miles one way; its inventory length is 3.8 miles. Google walking directions add about 0.2 mile each way from Derrick alternate parking. Twice the GIS trail plus the connector is 7.25 miles before the final spring approach, so 7.5 miles is the planning budget rather than an exact GPS total.

🌟 Recommended · Horton Spring jog/hike

One day, in order.

Leave the Phoenix area at 5:20 am, jog/hike at a moving average of 20 min/mile, and allow 20 minutes total for breaks. The roughly seven-mile outing has about 1,300 feet of estimated gain; budget up to 7.5 miles. Target a return around 11:00–11:10, followed by lunch around noon. There is no extra hiking buffer.

  • 5:20 am

    Leave Phoenix

    Budget 2 hr 30 min driving and a 15-minute charging allowance. Aim for the Payson stop around 7:10–7:25 am, then continue to Derrick parking. Coffee and a restroom visit can overlap charging if service is quick; extra time shifts the hike and lunch later. Starting the Model Y at 100% may let you skip that charging stop if navigation confirms enough reserve. Your actual starting point and traffic determine the drive.

  • ~7:10 am

    Charge & coffee in Payson

    Budget 15 minutes charging at the Payson Supercharger. The Starbucks by Bashas’ is about two minutes away on foot and lists a restroom. Coffee and bathroom time can overlap charging if the counter is open and service is quick; allow extra if needed. See the stop, walking map and hours ↓

  • 8:05 am

    Park at Derrick

    Take 15 minutes to change shoes, organize water and get ready. The five-minute connection to Horton Creek Trailhead is included in the 7–7.5-mile car-to-car planning budget.

  • 8:20 am

    Jog/hike to the spring

    At 20 min/mile, seven to 7.5 miles takes 2 hr 20–30 moving. Add 20 minutes for pauses, a snack and photos: 2 hr 40–50 elapsed. Hike the climbs and jog comfortable sections. Turn back sooner if tired.

  • 11:00–11:10 am

    Back at the car

    Target finish at jog/hike pace. Change shoes and head toward Payson promptly. There is no spare hiking buffer before the lunch drive; a longer spring approach, slower pace or extra stop shifts lunch later. Turn back early if the remaining route will exceed the window.

  • After the hike

    Drive to Payson

    Allow about 40 minutes driving to Duza’s Kitchen plus 20 minutes for parking, a possible wait and delays. Derrick Trailhead → Duza’s directions ↗. This is a planning allowance; check the actual route and traffic.

  • ~Noon–12:10

    Lunch at Duza’s Kitchen

    Block 60–75 minutes, through ordering, eating and paying. View Duza’s Kitchen ↗. Confirm current hours and kitchen cutoff; the provisional close is 2 pm. Macky’s Grill ↗ is the backup for a later lunch.

  • 1:00–1:25 pm

    Finish lunch & check charge

    If Tesla navigation calls for a top-up, budget 15 minutes charging plus up to ten minutes for a queue or detour. This is separate from lunch; restaurant charging is not confirmed. Without a stop, head home after the 60–75-minute lunch, around 1:00–1:25. With it, expect to leave Payson around 1:15–1:50. Allow longer if navigation predicts more charging.

  • 3:00–4:00 pm

    Home in Phoenix

    Allow roughly two hours driving from Payson to your Phoenix-area destination. A 1:00 departure puts you home around 3:00; a 2:00 departure around 4:00. These are provisional travel allowances, not live journey times.

The only adjustment

Turn back sooner if tired.

Stay together and retrace the same trail whenever the group has had enough. There is no alternate loop or shortcut planned. If keeping lunch near noon matters, check your progress during the climb and turn around by about 9:40 am if you have not reached the spring. Allow at least as much time to return as you have spent getting out, plus remaining breaks; turn earlier if the group is slowing.
