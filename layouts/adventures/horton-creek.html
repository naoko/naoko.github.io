Pace × distance + breaks

Put lunch on the clock.

These are planning calculations, not predicted hiking speeds. Pace is moving pace averaged over the whole route; stops are added separately. Plan to leave the trailhead by 11:00 am, allowing about 40 minutes to Payson and 20 minutes for parking and delays.

The five-mile row uses a chosen turnaround distance; the spring row uses Travelness’s published 8.6 miles. These calculations include more time than a fast hiker’s trip report and do not assume the guide’s 3–4 hour completion estimate will suit every group. Early starts can mean hiking in darkness: check local sunrise, bring headlamps, and choose the shorter route if the group wants a daylight start. Choose a pace that works for everyone in the group.

  1. 01
    4:45 am · provisional

    Leave Phoenix

    For the five-mile plan, budget 2 hr 30 min to the trailhead via AZ-87, Payson and AZ-260, plus 30 minutes for a charging stop and 15 minutes to get ready. That gives an 8:00 am start. Origin and live navigation may shift this substantially.

  2. 02
    8:00–11:00 am

    Walk beside the creek

    At 2 mph, 5 miles plus 30 minutes of stops finishes at 11:00. With an 8:00 start and 15 minutes of outbound stops, turn around by 9:30 or 2.5 miles, whichever comes first. Slower pace means a nearer turnaround.

  3. 03
    Spring option · about 2:40 am departure

    Allow a full morning

    With the same 3 hr 15 min travel, charge and setup allowance, leave around 2:40 am to start walking at 5:55. At 2 mph, 8.6 miles plus 45 minutes of breaks takes 5 hr 03 min, finishing around 10:58. This means a substantial walk in darkness. For a later start, choose the shorter creek walk or move lunch later and use the backup restaurant.

  4. 04
    11:00 am → noon

    Back to Payson

    Change shoes, drive back, and aim to be seated around noon. Return toward Phoenix after lunch and any required charge; provisionally home around 3:30–4:30 pm, depending on origin, traffic and charging.

03 / Pack

A November morning

Bring warm layers, headlamps for early starts, water, food, an offline map and shoes with grip. Wet leaves, rocks and possible ice can slow the walk. Carry drinking water; treat any creek water.
