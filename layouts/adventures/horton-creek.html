The five-mile row is a calculation for a chosen turnaround distance. Spring-route calculations are withheld until the GPS distances are verified. Early starts can mean hiking in darkness: check local sunrise, bring headlamps, and choose the shorter route if the group wants a daylight start. Choose a pace that works for everyone in the group.

  1. 01
    4:45 am · provisional

    Leave Phoenix

    For the five-mile plan, budget 2 hr 30 min to the trailhead via AZ-87, Payson and AZ-260, plus 30 minutes for a charging stop and 15 minutes to get ready. That gives an 8:00 am start. Origin and live navigation may shift this substantially.

  2. 02
    8:00–11:00 am

    Walk beside the creek

    At 2 mph, 5 miles plus 30 minutes of stops finishes at 11:00. With an 8:00 start and 15 minutes of outbound stops, turn around by 9:30 or 2.5 miles, whichever comes first. Slower pace means a nearer turnaround.

  3. 03
    Spring option · timing pending

    Verify the track first

    Choose the spring route after reviewing its GPS map. Once its full distance is verified, calculate walking time as distance divided by the group’s moving pace, then add breaks. Work backward from the 11:00 am trailhead departure and allow time for the drive, charging and setup.

  4. 04
    11:00 am → noon

    Back to Payson

    Change shoes, drive back, and aim to be seated around noon. Return toward Phoenix after lunch and any required charge; provisionally home around 3:30–4:30 pm, depending on origin, traffic and charging.
