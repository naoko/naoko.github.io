Five trip shapes

What each option means.

These descriptions explain the format without telling the group which one to prefer.

01

Montana · 3 days

Chasing Turquoise

Short Glacier

Fly in Friday, run Cracker Lake and Grinnell Glacier on Saturday, and return Sunday. The entire trail commitment sits in one large 23–24 mile day from a single Many Glacier base.

  1. MorningCracker Lake on AllTrails ↗ · 12.6–12.8 mi · +1,400 ft
  2. AfternoonGrinnell Glacier on AllTrails ↗ · 11 mi · +2,000 ft
  3. Full day23.6–23.8 mi · about +3,400 ft
  • Structure: 2 hotel nights, rental car, one active trail day.
  • Source note: AllTrails provides the linked route figures; Cracker Lake varies by 0.2 mile between sources.

02

Montana · 4 days

Glacier Extended

Combined plan

Keep the two signature Chasing Turquoise trails, but split them across two days. Both trailheads are in the Many Glacier area, so the group can keep one lodging base and avoid adding a Logan Pass–Avalanche driving loop.

  1. Day 1 Arrive and drive to Many Glacier.
  2. Day 2Cracker Lake on AllTrails ↗ · 12.6–12.8 mi · +1,400 ft
  3. Day 3Grinnell Glacier on AllTrails ↗ · 11 mi · +2,000 ft
  4. Day 4 Breakfast, drive to FCA and fly home.

Feasible from a Many Glacier base: this version totals 23.6–23.8 miles and about 3,400 feet of gain over two trail days. It is the same trail commitment as Chasing Turquoise, with recovery overnight instead of stacking both hikes into Saturday. Recheck 2027 road access, parking and trail conditions before booking.

03

Montana · 4–5 days

Marcela’s Glacier

Original poster outline

A broader National Park vacation that moves through several park areas and mixes hiking with sightseeing. Using the three named trails makes the active portion measurable: 19.9 miles and 3,243 feet of gain across three trail days.

  1. Day 1 Arrive in Kalispell, check in and explore Lake McDonald.
  2. Day 2Going-to-the-Sun Road and Hidden Lake Overlook on AllTrails ↗ · 2.8 mi · +538 ft
  3. Day 3Avalanche Lake on AllTrails ↗ · 6.1 mi · +705 ft
  4. Day 4Explore Many Glacier and hike Grinnell Glacier on AllTrails ↗ · 11 mi · +2,000 ft
  5. Day 5, optional Two Medicine or St. Mary Falls, then head home.

Rough budget: about $1,400–$2,600 per person

Assumes four travelers, two rooms, three or four nights, and travel from Phoenix. Every line except the 2027 boat prices and current park fee is a planning allowance.

04

Italy · ≈9 days away

Dolomites

Lisa’s Rogue guided highlights

Five supported day runs in different Dolomites areas, connected by professional drivers rather than one continuous Alta Via 1 traverse. Rogue supplies two running guides, logistics staff, six nights of lodging, most meals, gondolas and Venice transfers.

  • Published route: 41–44 miles and 12,800–14,200 feet of gain over five run days.
  • Lisa’s proposal price: $5,600. With the existing $1,200–$1,500 airfare estimate, the early total is about $6,800–$7,100 per person before a buffer hotel, insurance, tips, alcohol, personal spending and one excluded dinner.
  • Available 2027 dates: July 14–20 and September 5–11. Rogue currently lists both departures at $5,795; flights are explicitly not included, and no August departure is currently published.

05

France · Italy · Switzerland · ≈8 days away

Best of Mont Blanc

60-mile highlights route

A five-day, supported self-guided line from Les Contamines through Italy to La Fouly, followed by a Lac Blanc finale. It is the already-published 56–65 mile proposal, not the full ≈100-mile Tour du Mont Blanc.

  • Working route: 56–65 miles and 18,000–23,000 feet of gain.
  • Lodging: one refuge, one inn/gîte and three hotel nights; a pre-start night is additional.
  • Early total: about $2,650–$3,800 per person. This combines roughly $1,350–$2,150 for the converted land allowance, $1,200–$1,500 estimated Phoenix–Geneva airfare and $100–$150 for round-trip Geneva–Chamonix ground transfers. Insurance and extra nights remain excluded.
  • Planning reality: no operator has accepted this exact custom shape and no beds or transfers are held.
