Five trip shapes

What each option means.

These descriptions explain the format without telling the group which one to prefer.

01

Montana · 3 days

Chasing Turquoise

Short Glacier

Fly in Friday, run Cracker Lake and Grinnell Glacier on Saturday, and return Sunday. The entire trail commitment sits in one large 23–24 mile day from a single Many Glacier base.

  • Structure: 2 hotel nights, rental car, one active trail day.
  • Planning reality: the route is developed; 2027 flights, car, room availability and full per-person total are not.

02

Montana · 4 days

Glacier Extended

Combined plan

Keep Chasing Turquoise intact, then add two of the strongest compact pieces from Marcela’s outline: Hidden Lake Overlook and Avalanche Lake. They can share one Going-to-the-Sun Road day without turning the trip into a multi-hotel park tour, but this is a full sightseeing-and-hiking day rather than recovery.

  1. Day 1 Arrive and drive to Many Glacier.
  2. Day 2 Cracker Lake + Grinnell Glacier, 23–24 miles.
  3. Day 3 Hidden Lake Overlook, 2.8 mi / +460 ft round trip, then Avalanche Lake, 4.6 mi / +500 ft round trip.
  4. Day 4 Breakfast, drive to FCA and fly home.

Official Hidden Lake figures ↗ · Official Avalanche Lake figures ↗ · Avalanche Lake photos on AllTrails ↗

Feasible, but access-sensitive: the two hikes total about 7.4 miles and 960 feet of gain, on top of a long scenic driving loop from Many Glacier. In 2026, Logan Pass private parking was limited to three hours and the park shuttle did not serve Avalanche Lake. The group should recheck 2027 rules, start early, and be willing to drop either hike if parking, weather or recovery changes the day.

03

Montana · 4–5 days

Marcela’s Glacier

Original poster outline

A broader National Park vacation that moves through several park areas and mixes hiking with sightseeing. The source is Marcela’s printed proposal; it is an outline rather than a measured itinerary, so the budget below uses explicit planning assumptions.

  1. Day 1 Arrive in Kalispell, check in and explore Lake McDonald.
  2. Day 2 Going-to-the-Sun Road, Logan Pass and Hidden Lake Overlook.
  3. Day 3 Avalanche Lake hike or Lake McDonald boat tour.
  4. Day 4 Many Glacier boat tour and hike toward Grinnell Glacier.
  5. Day 5, optional Two Medicine or St. Mary Falls, then head home.

Rough budget: about $1,400–$2,600 per person

Assumes four travelers, two rooms, three or four nights, and travel from Phoenix. Every line except the 2027 boat prices and current park fee is a planning allowance.

04

Italy · ≈9 days away

Dolomites

Lisa’s Rogue guided highlights

Five supported day runs in different Dolomites areas, connected by professional drivers rather than one continuous Alta Via 1 traverse. Rogue supplies two running guides, logistics staff, six nights of lodging, most meals, gondolas and Venice transfers.

  • Published route: 41–44 miles and 12,800–14,200 feet of gain over five run days.
  • Lisa’s proposal price: $5,600. With the existing $1,200–$1,500 airfare estimate, the early total is about $6,800–$7,100 per person before a buffer hotel, insurance, tips, alcohol, personal spending and one excluded dinner.
  • Current price check: Rogue now lists $5,795 for July 14–20 and September 5–11, 2027. Flights are explicitly not included, and no August departure is currently published.

05

France · Italy · Switzerland · ≈8 days away

Best of Mont Blanc

60-mile highlights route

A five-day, supported self-guided line from Les Contamines through Italy to La Fouly, followed by a Lac Blanc finale. It is the already-published 56–65 mile proposal, not the full ≈100-mile Tour du Mont Blanc.

  • Working route: 56–65 miles and 18,000–23,000 feet of gain.
  • Lodging: one refuge, one inn/gîte and three hotel nights; a pre-start night is additional.
  • Planning reality: no operator has accepted this exact custom shape and no beds or transfers are held.

    Days awayWhat is the maximum number of calendar days, including flights?

    Trip formatVacation with running, or a running journey with vacation around it?

    Room standardAre shared rooms and shared bathrooms acceptable for any night?
