Five trip shapes

What each option means.

These descriptions explain the format without telling the group which one to prefer.

01

Montana · 3 days

Chasing Turquoise

Short Glacier

Fly in Friday, run Cracker Lake and Grinnell Glacier on Saturday, and return Sunday. The entire trail commitment sits in one large 23–24 mile day from a single Many Glacier base.

  • Structure: 2 hotel nights, rental car, one active trail day.
  • Planning reality: the route is developed; 2027 flights, car, room availability and full per-person total are not.

02

Montana · 4 days

Glacier Extended

Combined plan

Keep Chasing Turquoise intact, then add the strongest compact piece of Marcela’s outline: Going-to-the-Sun Road, Logan Pass and Hidden Lake Overlook. That creates a second, lighter trail day without turning the trip into a multi-hotel park tour.

  1. Day 1 Arrive and drive to Many Glacier.
  2. Day 2 Cracker Lake + Grinnell Glacier, 23–24 miles.
  3. Day 3 Going-to-the-Sun Road + Hidden Lake Overlook, planning estimate ≈2.7–3 miles.
  4. Day 4 Breakfast, drive to FCA and fly home.

This four-day combination is new on this page. The added route, road timing and elevation need current-source verification before booking.

03

Montana · 4–5 days

Marcela’s Glacier

Original poster outline

A broader National Park vacation that moves through several park areas and mixes hiking with sightseeing. The source is Marcela’s printed proposal; it is an outline, not a priced or measured itinerary.

  1. Day 1 Arrive in Kalispell, check in and explore Lake McDonald.
  2. Day 2 Going-to-the-Sun Road, Logan Pass and Hidden Lake Overlook.
  3. Day 3 Avalanche Lake hike or Lake McDonald boat tour.
  4. Day 4 Many Glacier boat tour and hike toward Grinnell Glacier.
  5. Day 5, optional Two Medicine or St. Mary Falls, then head home.

The poster suggests Lake McDonald Lodge, Many Glacier Hotel, Glacier Park Lodge, Whitefish or Columbia Falls; it does not assign a hotel to each night or specify prices, exact trail variants, mileage or elevation.

04

Italy · ≈8 days away

Dolomites

Runcation self-guided Express

Five point-to-point Alta Via 1 stages with four rifugio nights, daily bag movement, a detailed itinerary and operator GPX. The group moves at its own pace, but there is no guide making weather, navigation or exit decisions.

  • Published route: 58.5–63.5 miles and 15,900–17,900 feet of gain.
  • Published package: $2,195 based on two travelers; flights, pre/post hotels and ground access are additional.
  • 2027 constraint: the exact date window and any private room or ensuite request still need confirmation.

05

France · Italy · Switzerland · ≈8 days away

Best of Mont Blanc

60-mile highlights route

A five-day, supported self-guided line from Les Contamines through Italy to La Fouly, followed by a Lac Blanc finale. It is the already-published 56–65 mile proposal, not the full ≈100-mile Tour du Mont Blanc.

  • Working route: 56–65 miles and 18,000–23,000 feet of gain.
  • Lodging: one refuge, one inn/gîte and three hotel nights; a pre-start night is additional.
  • Planning reality: no operator has accepted this exact custom shape and no beds or transfers are held.

Before choosing

Four answers will narrow the field.

A vote is more useful after everyone answers the same practical questions privately.

  1. 01

    Days awayWhat is the maximum number of calendar days, including flights?

  2. 02

    Comfortable budgetWhat total per-person range feels comfortable, before optional upgrades?

  3. 03

    Trip formatVacation with running, or a running journey with vacation around it?

  4. 04

    Room standardAre shared rooms and shared bathrooms acceptable for any night?
